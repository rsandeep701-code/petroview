# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 26, 2026

## PetroView Privacy Policy

PetroView is committed to protecting your privacy.

### Information We Collect

PetroView does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

### LAS Files

PetroView allows you to open LAS files stored on your device.

- All file processing is performed locally on your device.
- Your LAS files are never uploaded to any server.
- PetroView does not access your files unless you explicitly select them.

### Internet Connection

PetroView works completely offline.

No internet connection is required for viewing or analyzing LAS files.

### Data Sharing

PetroView does not sell, rent, or share your personal information or files with any third party.

### Permissions

PetroView only requests the permissions necessary to allow you to select and open LAS files stored on your device.

### Third-Party Services

PetroView does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.

### Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

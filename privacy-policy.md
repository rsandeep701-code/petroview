# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 26, 2026

## PetroView Privacy Policy

PetroView is committed to protecting your privacy.

### Information Collection

PetroView does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

### LAS Files

All LAS files are processed locally on your device.

Your files are never uploaded to any server.

### Internet

PetroView works completely offline.

No internet connection is required to view or analyze LAS files.

### Permissions

PetroView only requests permissions necessary for selecting and opening LAS files stored on your device.

### Data Sharing

PetroView does not sell, share, or transmit your data.

### Third-Party Services

PetroView does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.
